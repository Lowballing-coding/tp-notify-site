Set Up in About Two Minutes

No app store. TP Notify runs from your Home Screen like an app and works on iPhone and Android.

  1. 1

    Create an Account

    Start with a free month, or pick a plan and pay securely with Stripe for instant alerts from day one.

  2. 2

    Add the App to Your Home Screen

    Open the app link on your phone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and sign in with your new account.

  3. 3

    Turn On Notifications

    Allow notifications once. From then on, every on-sale alert lands on your lock screen.

Questions

Is There a Free Version?

Every new account gets a free month, with no card needed. During it, Tickets on Sale alerts reach you 30 minutes late, and On Sale Soon and Check Manually alerts arrive instantly. After the month, alerts stop until you choose a plan. Paid plans get every alert the moment it happens.

Do You Sell Tickets?

No. TP Notify only sends alerts. You buy your tickets yourself on FIXR.

Are You Affiliated With Timepiece or FIXR?

No. TP Notify is an independent service and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Timepiece or FIXR.

Will I Always Get the Alert Instantly?

TP Notify checks about once a minute and sends an alert as soon as FIXR lists an event as on sale. It depends on FIXR's listing and on your phone's connection, so we can't guarantee you'll get a ticket, or that every alert arrives.

What Phone Do I Need?

An iPhone on iOS 16.4 or newer, or an Android phone. On iPhone you add the app to your Home Screen first, as notifications only work from there.

How Do I Cancel, and Can I Get a Refund?

You can cancel anytime from Settings in the app and keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. If you cancel within 14 days of your first payment, you get a refund minus the days you used. See the Refund Policy for the details.

What Do You Store About Me?

Your email, your name if you give one, and your phone's push address so alerts can reach it. Payments are handled by Stripe. Nothing is sold. See the Privacy Policy for the details.

Don't Find Out After It Sells Out

Get the alert while tickets are still there to buy.
