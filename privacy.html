Who We Share It With

We use these providers to run TP Notify. They only handle your data for that purpose.

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in. Data is stored in Ireland (eu-west-1).
  • Stripe: payments and billing.
  • Cloudflare: Turnstile checks that sign-ups and sign-ins come from a real person, using technical details such as your IP address and browser. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts website visits (pages viewed, rough location, device type) without cookies.
  • GitHub (Microsoft): hosts the website and the app. It may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address.
  • Oracle Cloud: a server in the UK that checks FIXR and sends your alerts.
  • Apple, Google and Mozilla push services: these deliver notifications to your phone. Alert content is encrypted in transit.

We may also share data if the law requires it, or to protect our rights.

Data on Your Own Device

The app saves some information on your phone so it works properly: your sign-in session, your theme choice, your alert history, and a list of alerts you have deleted. This stays on your device. We do not use cookies for tracking.

Moving Data Outside the UK

Some of our providers, such as Stripe, GitHub, Cloudflare and the push services, may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards these providers use, such as approved contract terms or recognised data-transfer schemes.

How Long We Keep It

  • Your account and device data: for as long as your account is open. If you close your account or ask us to delete it, we delete your profile, devices and nickname within 30 days.
  • Alert records (the log of which alerts were sent to whom): 90 days, then they are deleted automatically.
  • Payment and accounting records: 6 years, as UK tax rules require. Stripe keeps its own records under its policy.

Your Rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your data;
  • limit or stop how we use it;
  • give you your data in a portable format;
  • object to uses based on our legitimate interests.

You can turn off notifications in your phone's settings at any time. To use any of your rights, email TPNotify@outlook.com. We will reply within one month. There is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113. We would like the chance to help first, so please contact us.

Keeping Your Data Safe

Access to the database is limited by security rules, so you can only see your own data. Connections are encrypted. Payment details are handled by Stripe. No system is perfectly secure. If there is a breach that puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

Children

TP Notify is not for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
