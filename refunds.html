Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

TP Notify is run by Peter Ellul, a sole trader based in England ("we", "us"). You can contact us at TPNotify@outlook.com, or write to us at 51 New North Road, Exeter, EX4 4EP.

This policy explains how cancelling and refunds work. It does not affect your legal rights as a consumer.

What You Are Buying

TP Notify is a subscription service. It sends push alerts to your phone when an event on the Timepiece listing is shown as on sale on FIXR. It does not sell or buy tickets.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel at any time. Open Settings in the app and tap Manage Subscription, which opens your billing page on Stripe. Or email TPNotify@outlook.com and we will cancel it for you.
  • When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. You will not be charged again.
  • We do not give refunds for time left in a period you have already paid for, except in the cases below.

Your 14-Day Right to Cancel (First Payment)

Under UK consumer law, you can cancel a new subscription within 14 days of buying it and get a refund.

Because TP Notify starts working as soon as your payment goes through, you are asking us to start the service straight away. If you then cancel within the 14 days, we will refund your payment minus a fair amount for the days you have already used. We work this out like this:

  • Monthly plan: the first month's £3.99 divided over 30 days.
  • 3 Months plan: the £11.99 divided over 90 days.

Examples:

  • You cancel the Monthly plan after 5 days: you pay £0.67 for the days used and we refund £3.32.
  • You cancel the 3 Months plan after 10 days: you pay £1.33 for the days used and we refund £10.66.

To use this right, email TPNotify@outlook.com from the address on your account within 14 days of your first payment. This right applies to your first payment on a plan, not to later renewals.

Other Cases Where We Will Refund You

We will refund you (in full, or for the part of the period affected) if:

  • we charge you by mistake, or charge you after you cancelled;
  • the service is unavailable for more than 48 hours in a row because of a fault on our side, in which case we may offer a refund or extra time at your choice;
  • we have to stop the service permanently, in which case we refund the unused part of your current paid period.

What Is Not a Reason for a Refund

TP Notify does not guarantee that you will get a ticket, or that every alert arrives. Alerts depend on FIXR's listing and on your phone's connection and settings. We cannot refund you because an event sold out, because FIXR changed an event, or because your phone blocked a notification.

Failed Payments

If a renewal payment fails, Stripe may try again for a short time. While your payment is overdue, your alerts are paused. They start again once the payment goes through. You can update your card at any time.

Free Access

The owner may give some people free access ("exempt" accounts). Free access can be changed to a paid plan. We will tell you before that happens, and you can say no and stop using the service.

Price Changes

If we change our prices, we will tell you at least 30 days before your next payment. If you do not want to pay the new price, you can cancel before it takes effect.

How Refunds Are Paid

Refunds go back to the card or payment method you used, through Stripe. They usually reach you within 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank.
